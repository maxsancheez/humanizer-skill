Remote work has changed how a lot of people live, and it affects how they feel about their jobs. Flexibility seems to help output, though the evidence varies by team.

Offices still matter. Long commutes are what people want gone. A remote team runs on trust and on tools that work, and it needs a steady rhythm of check-ins so nobody drifts.

What should leaders do? Judge people on what they finish, not on when they are online, and give the team reasons to talk to each other that are not status updates.
